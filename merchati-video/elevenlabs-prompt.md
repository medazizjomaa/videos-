## MASTER PROMPT (global style — applies to every scene)

Create a 65-second vertical (9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps) motion-design promo video for **MERCHATI**, a Tunisian
AI employee that answers customers' Instagram, Messenger and website messages in Tunisian Darija, takes
e-commerce orders, books restaurant tables, and alerts the owner on Telegram. Also deliver a 1:1 (1080×1080)
version with the same scenes re-laid out (headline on the left, visual on the right).

**Style:** clean, premium SaaS motion design (Apple / Linear / Stripe launch-video feel). Light mode only.
Flat UI mock-ups (iPhone frames, chat threads, dashboards, notification cards) floating on a soft background,
smooth spring animations, gentle camera push-ins, masked text reveals, no 3D characters, no real people,
no stock footage. Everything must look like a real product UI, crisp and readable.

**Background (every scene):** very light blue-grey (#F4F7FC) with two slowly drifting soft glows
(brand blue #097CF1 top-left at ~15% opacity, cyan #15EFF8 bottom-right at ~14% opacity), a faint 72-px grid
that slowly scrolls diagonally and fades out toward the edges, and a soft vignette (#E9EEF7) on the borders.

**Brand colors:** blue #097CF1, navy #011A4D (text), cyan #15EFF8, soft blue #3BA0FF. Body text #0B1B3F.
Success green #22C55E, alert red #F0293E, amber #FFB547. Cards: white #FFFFFF, 1-px borders rgba(11,27,63,0.07),
large soft shadows, 18–28 px rounded corners.

**Typography:** headlines in *Plus Jakarta Sans* ExtraBold (800), tight letter-spacing (−3.5%), 78 px on 9:16,
navy, left-aligned at the top (x = 76 px, y ≈ 150 px), max 2 lines. The highlighted words (shown in
*italics* below) use a blue→teal gradient (#097CF1 → #00A6D6). UI text in *Inter*.

**Headline animation (every scene):** word-by-word masked reveal — each word slides up from behind an
invisible mask with a snappy spring, 3 frames apart. When a headline is replaced, the words slide out upward
in reverse order, then the new headline slides in.

**Scene transitions** (14 frames ≈ 0.45 s; the outgoing scene keeps moving underneath):
iris (circle opening from the center), wipe-up, wipe-left, or zoom-fade, as indicated per scene.

**Logo:** use the uploaded MERCHATI logo and icon exactly as provided — never redraw, recolor or distort them.
The icon is a blue speech bubble with a navy robot face and two cyan eyes.

**Language of on-screen text:** Tunisian Darija written in Latin letters ("Franco-Arabe", numbers 3/7/9 for
Arabic sounds, e.g. *wa7ed*, *ta3mer*, *3la*) mixed with French. Copy every on-screen text **exactly** as
written below, including spelling and accents. Prices in TND / DT.

**Do not:** add other brands, invent extra text, show real people's faces, use dark mode, use English
headlines, add subtitles, or add a watermark.
