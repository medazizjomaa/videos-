Timed to the video. Arabic script is for TTS voices (ElevenLabs, or Microsoft `ar-TN-HediNeural` / `ar-TN-ReemNeural`); Arabizi is for a human voice actor.
Tone: calm, confident, a bit warm. Let each line land on its scene, and leave the gaps for music.

## How to add it in CapCut
1. Import `out/merchati-9x16.mp4` (or `-1x1`).
2. Record each line with *Voiceover*, or generate it with *Text to speech* using an Arabic voice, and place each clip at its time above.
3. Duck the music by about 8 dB under the voice.
